## 🏗️ Architectural Philosophy

Welcome to my engineering lab. I specialize in designing scalable distributed systems, governing robust CI/CD pipelines, and enforcing strict architectural boundaries (**Control Plane vs. Data Planes**). My work is driven by a **Plan-First** methodology.

*   📐 **Domain-Driven Design (DDD):** Isolating business logic from infrastructure.
*   ⚙️ **Infrastructure as Code:** Governing deployments with rigorous CI/CD automation.
*   🔒 **Predictable State:** Ensuring transactional integrity across complex ecosystems.
